# Qdral Computer-Use Zero-Cost Constraint

Status: PLANNED
Date: 2026-10-03

P18 must not introduce a required paid browser API, paid computer-use service, paid cloud VM, paid relay tier, or paid model dependency. Local computer-use must remain usable with supported local browser/runtime components and the user's chosen AI client/model. Optional external providers may require their own user account, but no paid provider is required for Qdral's local security/runtime layer.
